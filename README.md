# Excel-Sales-Analysis
An Excel-based sales analysis project exploring revenue trends, product performance, and business opportunities.
Objective: Analyze 2,000 orders to understand where revenue comes from, which products and channels perform best, and how sales change over time.
Workbook Structure;
- **Cleaned Dataset**: the prepared data used for analysis
- **Pivot Analysis**: PivotTables behind each chart
- **Sales_Dashboard**: the interactive dashboard with slicers and date timelines
  
**Here are some of the key insights from the dashboard:**

 **Total Revenue:** $2.45M generated across 2,000 orders, with a total quantity of 5,552 units.
 **Average Order Value:** Approximately $1.23K per order, giving a quick view of the average revenue generated per transaction.
**Top-Performing Product:** The Gaming Laptop led product revenue at approximately $432.6K, followed by the Laptop Air 13 and Laptop Pro 14.
**Regional Performance:** The North region recorded the highest revenue at approximately $561.75K, followed by the Central region at $556.71K.
**Monthly Trends:** Revenue fluctuated throughout the year, with November recording the highest monthly revenue at approximately $274.37K. This is an area worth exploring further to understand what drove the increase.
